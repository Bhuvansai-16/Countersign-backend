# Playbook: significantly not as described (SNAD)

Goal: show the item delivered matches what was listed and what the buyer (or their agent) asked for.
Compare the listing description and attributes with the buyer's claim. When an intent receipt exists, quote the
principal's instruction and constraints and show the cart that was confirmed matches them.
Do not argue about the buyer's honesty; stick to documented facts. If the item may genuinely be wrong or damaged,
an offer (refund with return) is usually better than a fight.

[POL-SNAD-1] Seller Protection does not cover claims that an item is significantly not as described, so the evidence submitted decides these cases.
Source: https://www.paypal.com/us/legalhub/paypal/useragreement-full

[POL-SNAD-2] Sellers have 10 days to respond to a dispute.
Source: https://www.paypal.com/us/cshelp/article/HELP1083
