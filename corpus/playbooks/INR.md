# Playbook: item not received (INR)

Goal: prove the item was shipped to the address on the PayPal transaction and delivered.
Lead with the carrier, tracking number, ship date and delivery status. State that the delivery address matches
the shipping address on the PayPal order. If tracking is missing, say so plainly; do not imply delivery.
Keep the note short and chronological: order, payment, shipment, delivery, dispute.

[POL-INR-1] Sellers have 10 days to respond to a dispute; if the seller does not respond and the buyer escalates, PayPal can decide in the buyer's favour.
Source: https://www.paypal.com/us/cshelp/article/HELP1083

[POL-INR-2] PayPal accepts proof of shipment or delivery, such as a shipping receipt, tracking information or signature confirmation, as dispute evidence.
Source: https://www.paypal.com/us/cshelp/article/HELP1083

[POL-INR-3] Seller Protection for item-not-received claims requires tracking that shows the item was delivered to the address on the transaction details.
Source: https://www.paypal.com/us/legalhub/paypal/useragreement-full
