# Playbook: credit not processed

Goal: show the refund was already issued.
Give the refund amount, date and refund ID, and the original capture it relates to.

[POL-CNP-1] Proof of refund is accepted as dispute evidence.
Source: https://www.paypal.com/us/cshelp/article/HELP1083

[POL-CNP-2] Sellers have 10 days to respond to a dispute.
Source: https://www.paypal.com/us/cshelp/article/HELP1083
